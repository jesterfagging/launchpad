How __NAME__ works.

Launch a Solana coin in a few minutes: name it, give it a picture, pick where it trades and what its fees do, then approve one funding transfer from your wallet.

Launching a coin

  1. Connect your wallet from the header. Your wallet is your sign-in; nothing is created for you.
  2. Describe the coin on Launch: name, ticker, picture and, if you like, a description and links.
  3. Pick the venue and the launch mode. The venue decides the bonding curve and where the coin trades after it graduates; the mode decides where its creator fees go.
  4. Choose an optional first buy and review the exact cost: network fees, account rent and storage are listed before you sign.
  5. Approve the funding transfer. The coin is created from a launch wallet made only for it; anything the launch did not use comes back to your wallet.

Your coins, their fees and their payouts are under My coins.

Venues

Pump.fun

The coin starts on Pump.fun’s bonding curve and graduates to a PumpSwap pool. Pump.fun’s standard trading fees apply. Its creator fee is shared through Pump.fun’s own fee sharing, set when the coin is created and not changed afterwards.

Raydium

The coin starts on a Raydium LaunchLab bonding curve and graduates to a Raydium pool. A coin starts at __STARTS_RAYDIUM__ and graduates at a $50,000 market cap. A platform fee of 1% applies to buys and sells on the curve; the creator fee depends on the launch mode.

Meteora

The coin starts on Meteora’s dynamic bonding curve and graduates to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool. A coin starts at __STARTS_METEORA__ and graduates at a $50,000 market cap. The pool’s trading fee is 1.88%, shared between the platform and the coin’s creator or holders as described under Fees.

Launch modes

A launch mode decides what happens to a coin’s creator fees. It is chosen at launch and stays with the coin.

  • Creator — creator fees are paid to the creator’s wallet.
  • Holders — creator fees fund rewards for the coin’s top holders, paid out automatically once their share is large enough to be worth sending.
  • Gotchi — creator fees fill the prize pot of the Gotchi the coin is paired with.
  • AI Agent — creator fees fund an AI agent with a mission that researches approved sites, posts short updates and can propose small, capped treasury actions.
  • Diamond Hands (Pump.fun) — holder rewards that skip any wallet that sold since the last payout; every payout a wallet holds through grows its share.
  • Earned (Pump.fun) — creator fees wait in escrow and are released to the creator only if the coin holds its value through a probation period; otherwise they pay holders.
  • Insured (Pump.fun) — part of the creator fees builds a refund pot; if the coin has not graduated by its deadline, the pot refunds holders.
  • Market Maker (Pump.fun) — creator fees fund a bot that buys and sells from its own wallet to keep a target mix of SOL and the coin.
  • Liquidity Manager (Pump.fun) — creator fees accumulate until graduation, then add liquidity to the coin’s PumpSwap pool.

Transfer hooks

Meteora coins can carry transfer rules enforced by a transfer hook program on every transfer while the coin is on its curve, for example a blocklist or limits on early buys. Rules are chosen at launch and shown on the coin’s page. A hooked launch takes one more wallet approval and starts without a first buy.

Features

Launches. Every coin launched here gets a number, in order; the first 100 are __NAME__’s Founding coins. Launches lists them all with search, filters and sorting, and Stats shows the biggest coins, the most traded today and the latest graduations.

Ticker lock. A new coin’s ticker and name are locked here for 3 days. After that they stay locked while the coin is established: a $20K market cap (or graduated and at $10K) and traded in the last 7 days. A ticker that looks like an earlier coin’s (P3PE for PEPE) or a picture copied from an earlier coin is allowed, but the coin page flags it.

Bundle check. About a minute and a half after a launch, __NAME__ reads the coin’s first buyers from the chain. Buyers funded by the creator’s wallets, or by each other, are shown on the coin page as linked, with how much of the supply they hold now. It is a pointer, not proof: check the wallets yourself.

Summit. Every day at 00:00 UTC one coin is drawn to hold the summit for 24 hours, on the home page and on Summit. The draw is random; a bigger market cap, more trading in the last 24 hours and being new raise a coin’s odds. Coins flagged as copies, or whose linked early buyers hold 1% or more of the supply, are left out.

Community takeover. If a coin’s creator walks away, its holders can ask for a new logo, banner, links and description on Takeover. A wallet holding 0.5% or more of the coin signs the request (a message, not a transaction, and free), and __NAME__’s owner approves it. Only how the coin looks here changes; the coin itself stays as it is.

Wallets

You sign in and pay with your own wallet. Each coin is created from a launch wallet made only for it, so its fees, payouts and refunds are kept apart from every other coin. Those wallets are held by the servers that run __NAME__; fee collection and payouts run automatically and every transaction can be checked on a block explorer.

Risk

Coins launched here are created by their creators, not by __NAME__. Prices can go to zero, rewards depend on trading activity, and nothing on this site is investment advice. Read the Terms before you launch or trade.
