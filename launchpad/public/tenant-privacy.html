What __NAME__ keeps

  • Your wallet address and the signatures that prove you control it, to sign you in.
  • Your launches: what you entered, the pictures you uploaded, the wallets made for each coin and their transactions.
  • Your profile, if you set one: a username, a picture and a short bio.
  • Request logs: network address, time and the page or action, kept briefly for security and rate limits.

Public by design

Blockchain transactions, coin names, tickers, pictures, descriptions, links and creator wallets are public and permanent once published or confirmed. __NAME__ cannot delete anything from the blockchain or from permanent storage.

Providers

To work, __NAME__ sends the data each request needs to Solana RPC providers, venue services (Pump.fun, Raydium, Meteora), price and routing services (such as Jupiter and DexScreener) and storage networks for coin metadata. They apply their own policies. __NAME__ runs on hosted servers operated for it. __NAME__ does not sell your data and shows no third-party ads.

Cookies

One sign-in cookie keeps your session; a few settings (such as the light or dark theme) are kept in your browser. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Retention

Account and launch records are kept while the service runs, because fees and payouts depend on them. Request logs are kept for a short time. Records that the law requires to be kept are kept for as long as it requires.

Your choices

You can edit or clear your profile at any time and disconnect your wallet. For anything else, contact __NAME__ through the official links in the site footer.
