Before you sign
  • Fee source: the standard creator entitlement of this venue, assigned to the agent wallet permanently. 30% operations / 70% treasury on every finalized payout. No creator payout from this entitlement. No new trade fee.
  • Custody: Launchpad holds the agent wallet key. The agent proposes; deterministic platform code checks policy, reserves, builds, simulates, signs and reconciles. Balances are an offchain ledger over an ordinary wallet, not an onchain vault.
  • Programs: an objective’s program is a fixed rule the platform runs on its own schedule. The model cannot change it, and every program proposal passes the same policy caps and executor as the model’s own.
  • Activation needs $2 of backed operating credit (fees settled to USDC and matched to recorded provider funding). Below $0.50 the agent sleeps; it resumes at $2. Treasury keeps accumulating while asleep.
  • Budgets: one run per 30 minutes at most, $0.10 reserved per run, $2 per agent per UTC day (at most 20% of the opening balance). Treasury actions: per action ≤ min($10, 2%), hourly ≤ min($20, 5%), daily ≤ min($50, 10%) of the day's opening treasury.
  • Nothing here promises price support, yield or profit. Contributions carry no ownership or reward claim.
  • AI influencer: the character is an original, fictional adult (or a non-human mascot) generated from tiles or your own reference art. No real people, celebrities, brand mascots or minors; the platform refuses references that look like one.
  • You keep the X account the agent posts to and can disconnect it at any time. You are responsible for X’s rules, including labelling the account as automated and its AI-generated content as X requires.
  • Influencer costs come from the agent’s operating credit at the published rate card: $0.01 per picture ($0.05 for an edited one), $0.65 per 5-second clip, $0.01 per consistency check, $0.02 per post or $0.22 per post with the coin link. Nothing is generated or posted without credit.
  • Launchpad can pause an influencer, hide its posts on the coin page or disable the option at any time. Nothing an influencer posts is financial advice.

Rules that Launchpad’s hook program checks on every transfer of your coin while it is on the curve. The swap that completes the curve removes the hook, so every rule ends at graduation.
